Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Suite 102 Salon & Spa specialize in curly hair?

Yes. Suite 102 is Boynton Beach's go-to salon for curly hair, offering Deva Cuts and curl-specific styling from stylists who specialize in every curl pattern.

Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome, though calling ahead at 561.318.6113 helps us guarantee you a spot with your preferred stylist.

What are Suite 102's salon hours?

The salon is open Wednesday through Saturday, 9:30am to 9:00pm. We're closed Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday.

Can I call outside of salon hours?

Yes, calls are answered 7 days a week from 9am to 9pm, even on days the salon itself is closed.

How do I book an appointment?

Suite 102 books appointments by phone only. Call 561.318.6113 to schedule.

Where is Suite 102 Salon & Spa located?

Suite 102 Salon & Spa is located at 3960 Hypoluxo Rd, Boynton Beach, FL 33436.

How much does a haircut cost?

Women's haircuts start at $65, men's haircuts start at $40, and Deva curly cuts start at $95. Pricing varies with hair length, density, and technique — see our full services & pricing above.

Who owns Suite 102 Salon & Spa?

Suite 102 is a family-owned salon run by husband-and-wife team Carmel and Johnna Lobato, who opened the salon together in 2014.
